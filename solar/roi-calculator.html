Solar-specific assumptions

  • Electricity rate — use your actual tariff ($/kWh), not a national average; this drives the dollar value of every kWh offset.
  • Tax credit — reduces net cost as a percentage of system price; eligibility and basis rules vary and change over time, so confirm your situation separately before relying on the number.
