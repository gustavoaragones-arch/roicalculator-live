How these calculators differ

These calculators share some of the same underlying cost inputs because material, electricity, machine wear, and labor affect any 3D-printing operation. The calculations answer different business questions, however. The Business ROI Calculator models one printer and its return over time. The Print Farm ROI Calculator adds fleet capacity and utilization constraints. The Service Pricing Calculator works from the cost of a specific job to determine a price that meets a target margin.

Frequently asked questions

Which 3D printing calculator should I use if I have two printers?

Use the Print Farm ROI Calculator if you want to analyze fleet capacity and utilization across your printers together. Use the Business ROI Calculator if you'd rather treat the operation as single-printer economics — for example, evaluating one printer's numbers and assuming the second behaves the same way.
